## Project context

This work formed part of the project **Technology Pathways and Industry-University-Research Collaboration Strategies for Integrated PV-Storage Systems**, conducted as a strategic research and consulting project of the Chinese Academy of Engineering.

- **Period:** April 2023 to September 2024
- **Project type:** Engineering strategy research, technical and policy consulting, stakeholder research

Deploying PV-storage-DC-flexible building systems requires more than the availability of individual technologies. Regional implementation depends on local supply chains, building conditions, grid interaction, technical standards, market acceptance, and supporting policies. The project therefore connected evidence that was fragmented across policy documents, engineering cases, suppliers, design institutes, and research organisations.

## My contribution

- Developed the stakeholder survey framework and structured questions covering technology deployment, engineering cases, material supply, system integration, economic feasibility, standards, and policy support.
- Synthesised evidence from policy documents, technical literature, engineering cases, and stakeholder interviews.
- Served as a principal drafter of Chapter 2, organising the evidence into regional low-carbon development, foundations for PEDF buildings, and strategic opportunities.
