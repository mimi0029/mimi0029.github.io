## Project context

**Key Technologies and Application Demonstration of Novel Energy Systems for Zero-Carbon Retrofit of Existing Campuses** was conducted by Tianjin University with China Yangtze Power Co., Ltd. and Three Gorges Electric Energy Co., Ltd.

- **Period:** June 2023 to April 2025
- **Project type:** Industry-linked R&D, technology roadmap, integrated energy-system planning, intellectual-property development

Existing campuses contain buildings of different ages and functions, diverse heating, cooling, and electrical loads, and uneven renewable-energy resources. The project organised demand reduction, photovoltaics, BIPV, PVT, heat pumps, storage, DC distribution, flexible demand, and environmental-resource management into a coherent and transferable pathway.
