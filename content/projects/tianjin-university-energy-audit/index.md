## Project context

The project was conducted by Tianjin University and the APEC Sustainable Energy Center under the **2023 Energy Audit Plan for Key Energy-Using Units** issued by the Tianjin Municipal Development and Reform Commission.

- **Period:** December 2023 to July 2024
- **Audit year:** 2022, compared with 2021
- **Project type:** Regulatory energy audit, campus energy assessment, retrofit feasibility study

The audit converted energy bills, metering records, building inventories, equipment parameters, and management documents into a consistent and traceable energy baseline. The work also translated the audit findings into technically feasible conservation measures and quantified their energy, carbon, investment, and financial implications.

## My contribution

- Led a five-member team, defined the scope, allocated work, tracked progress, coordinated data requests, and integrated the final report.
- Conducted UAV-supported field investigation and contributed to site verification.
- Developed the campus energy-accounting framework for electricity, natural gas, purchased heat, and water across two campuses.
- Modelled retrofit scenarios and assessed energy savings, carbon reductions, investment, annual savings, payback, NPV, and IRR.
