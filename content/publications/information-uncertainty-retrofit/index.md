## Research question

Which building information should be acquired first, and how much information is sufficient for a stable retrofit decision?

## Method

The framework combines deep-learning-assisted facade information acquisition, building-performance simulation, surrogate modelling, global sensitivity analysis, NSGA-III optimisation, and decision-robustness assessment. A common workflow is applied across eight contrasting residential-building cases while preserving case-specific parameterisation.

## Selected evidence

- Building-information uncertainty is propagated to energy, comfort, carbon, and cost outcomes.
- Facade features are extracted and window-to-wall-ratio estimation error is quantified.
- Full-information regret measures decision loss under incomplete information.
- The analysis identifies information priorities and minimum sufficient information for different decision preferences.
