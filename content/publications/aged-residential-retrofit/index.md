## Research question

How can retrofit strategies be generated and screened efficiently while remaining interpretable and adaptable to heterogeneous aged residential communities?

## Method

The workflow represents 216 community archetypes through thermal, geometric, spatial, and clean-energy characteristics. Physics-based simulation, surrogate models, SHAP analysis, surrogate-assisted optimisation, and context-adaptive weighting connect performance prediction with strategy ranking and engineering pathways.

## Selected evidence

- 690,795 retrofit combinations were evaluated across 216 community archetypes.
- SHAP analysis revealed construction-period shifts in key retrofit drivers.
- Context-adaptive AHP-EWM weights integrated energy, carbon, PV, and life-cycle cost.
- Lower post-retrofit energy use did not necessarily imply higher whole-life viability.
