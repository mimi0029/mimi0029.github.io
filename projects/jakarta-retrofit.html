Data-Driven Adaptive Framework for Low-Carbon Retrofitting of Settlements in Jakarta

How can low-carbon retrofit decisions remain rigorous when settlement data, budgets, and climate conditions vary sharply?

The 3rd ISETS-ESCAP & 4th ISETS Global Competition of Youth Voice on Energy Transition

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Making retrofit choices under heterogeneous conditions

Low-carbon retrofit decisions can be difficult to compare when settlement information, building conditions, climate exposure, and available resources differ. The project explores a structured, data-driven process for evaluating options rather than relying on a single default retrofit solution.

02 / THE APPROACH

From data to adaptive decision support

The framework brings together four linked steps:

  1. 01
    Data acquisition and parameter extraction

    Organise settlement and building information for subsequent analysis.

  2. 02
    Baseline modelling and assessment

    Establish a basis for evaluating energy and thermal-comfort performance.

  3. 03
    Multi-objective scenario analysis

    Compare retrofit alternatives against multiple performance criteria.

  4. 04
    Adaptive decision support

    Use the analysis to inform retrofit choices under differing conditions.

PROJECT LENS

Not one retrofit for every settlement.

The project frames retrofit selection as a context-sensitive comparison, informed by building models and energy/comfort indicators.
